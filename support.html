Frequently Asked Questions
How can I track my order live?

Navigate to the Orders tab at the bottom. Active orders feature a live interactive GPS delivery map showing Captain Mostafa's moving scooter and 5 order milestones from kitchen prep to arrival.

What are the free delivery requirements?

Orders of 300 EGP or more qualify for automatic 100% Free Delivery anywhere in Alexandria! You can also redeem 300 loyalty points for a Free Delivery voucher in the Loyalty Hub.

How do I pay via Vodafone Cash or InstaPay?

During checkout or on our Payment Methods page, select Vodafone Cash or InstaPay. For mobile wallets, Captain will present our verified CBE QR code or verify your wallet transfer PIN.

Can I customize pizza crust and burger patties?

Yes! Tap any pizza or burger to choose patty weights (150g, 300g, 450g), cheeses, artisan stuffed crusts, and special cooking notes.
